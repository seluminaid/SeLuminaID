About SeLuminaID

SeLuminaID is a startup focused on developing simple software that makes everyday activities easier. We build tools that do one job clearly, work right in your browser, and don't ask you to create an account.

Simple

Each tool does one job clearly, so new users know what to do without a tutorial.

Practical

Built around real daily tasks, like making a QR code, shrinking a file, or merging a PDF.

Private

Files are processed in your browser wherever possible, not uploaded to a server.

Vision & Mission

Vision

To make everyday digital tasks simple for everyone.

Mission

  • Build simple tools that solve real, everyday problems.
  • Keep every product easy to understand, even for first-time users.
  • Respect user privacy by processing files in the browser wherever possible.
  • Keep improving the quality and reliability of every product.
